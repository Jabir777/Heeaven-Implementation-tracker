First time? How to prepare the sheet
  1. In your tracker Google Sheet add a tab named exactly Feed and a tab named Feed Log (the Heavens_Implementation_Control workbook already contains them).
  2. Share → General access → Anyone with the link → Viewer.
  3. Paste the link above and press Load sheet. The link is remembered on this device.
  4. To give management a ready-made link, add ?sheet= and your sheet link or ID to the end of this page’s address.
